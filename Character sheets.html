Character sheets. A persona, pointed at a colleague. Print at actual size: four cards per A4 sheet, cut along the dashed lines. The filled-in cards are starting points; swap in what you know about the real people you work with. The blank card is for your own.

Character sheet
Product Manager
Goals
Ship the roadmap item. Keep the promises made to leadership and customers.
Fears
Slipping a date in front of leadership. Surprises late in the cycle.
Perception · notices first
Dates, scope, customer impact. In that order.
Currency · what convinces them
Customer and revenue risk.
Notes · what you know about the real person
Say it like this
"This puts checkout conversion at risk. Fixing now costs 3 days; shipping as is risks about £X a day."

Developer
